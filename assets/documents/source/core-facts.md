# My AI Native Town — Core Facts

Core facts about the My AI Native Town AI Agent Training Program, Cohort 01, in Bhiwani and Hisar, Haryana. Updated 13 October 2026. Source: the program's public site repository and the Cohort 01 banners.

## My AI Native Town program overview

My AI Native Town is a 90-day AI Agent Training Program in Bhiwani and Hisar, Haryana, run online and offline. Students learn to build AI agents now and pay the program fee only after they are hired. The Hindi tagline is "पहले सीखो। फ़ीस नौकरी लगने के बाद।" (Learn first. Fee after you get a job.) The website is myainativetown.com.

The program is run by Hari Om Vashishtha (@hov8a), a Bhiwani-based builder who works in public and runs the YouTube channel @AgentforceWithHov8a. The program and its website are built in public.

The cohort builds one real "town agent" for the Bhiwani–Hisar district together, rather than separate demos. Every wrong answer the agent gives is logged where students can read it.

## How My AI Native Town differs from a normal course

My AI Native Town teaches by building: learners build agents again and again, not one single agent, until building one is routine. There are no pre-recorded videos: every session is live. There is no certificate at the end; students leave with a working AI agent. Homework is judged by whether the goal is met, not by hours spent. Every day is run as a project with a log entry in three parts: hypothesis, actions, expectations. All student work goes into a public build log that future employers can read.

## Local problems the town agent tackles

My AI Native Town starts from four questions specific to Bhiwani and Hisar, and each student aims to answer one in 90 days. For kirana and retail shops: can an agent move the day's udhaar (credit) from a shopkeeper's notebook onto his phone? For coaching institutes: can it answer the forty admission questions parents ask every evening, in Hindi? For government forms and schemes: can it tell someone which scheme they qualify for and which four papers to carry? For clinics: can it hold the appointment book without the receptionist changing how she works?

## How Cohort 01 starts: homework and two free sessions

Applications for My AI Native Town Cohort 01 are open. Every applicant first does a homework evaluation: a short voice interview and one written task, in which they pick a local business and write a hypothesis, actions and expectations plan for an AI agent. Doing the homework at all is the first filter, and it shows which track fits the applicant.

Applicants then attend two free sessions, two hours each, on alternate days. In them they build a first small agent for the business they chose. At the end of the second free session, each applicant decides whether to join.

## Cohort 01 seats and schedule

My AI Native Town Cohort 01 runs for 90 days after the free sessions. It uses a goal-oriented format, so there is no fixed end date or fixed number of sessions. Cohort 01 has 10 seats, held first come, first served, with no waitlist once they are filled.

Live sessions run on alternate days. Each session lasts 2 hours, with the mentor present. On the days between sessions, students learn on their own and build, logging each day as a project. The cohort does not pause for Diwali.

## Class format and location

My AI Native Town classes are online and offline. Offline classes are held in Bhiwani and Hisar, Haryana; the exact venue will be shared later. Students can also join online. Sessions are never recorded; a student who misses one catches up from the public build log.

## Fees: registration fee and program fee

My AI Native Town charges a ₹1,000 registration fee, which covers registration and the notary service for the student's written agreement. It is paid at the end of the second free session by those who decide to join, through the program's counselor; there is no online payment link. Before being hired, the student pays nothing more. The program fee is due only after the student is hired, and is paid as a share of salary. The exact share and installments are set with the counselor and recorded in the written agreement; they are not published.

## The pay-after-hire rules

In My AI Native Town, the program fee is due only when a student is "hired". A job offer of ₹3 LPA or more counts as hired. An offer below ₹3 LPA does not count. Self-employment and freelance work count only if they earn ₹3 LPA or more.

Being hired during the program counts, so the fee becomes due. A job that comes within 1 to 3 months of the program counts, as set in the student's written agreement. If the student is not hired, they do not pay the program fee.

## How the program fee is paid

The My AI Native Town program fee is paid in installments as a share of the student's salary. The share is mutually agreed between the student and the program. Every student signs a written agreement before joining that records these terms.

## Curriculum: the three layers

Over 90 days, the My AI Native Town cohort builds one AI agent for the Bhiwani–Hisar district in three layers. Layer 1, in the early sessions, is the category walk: writing down what the town actually needs before touching any AI model. Layer 2, in the middle sessions, adds tools, data and guardrails; the first wrong answers appear here and are kept and studied. Layer 3, in the final sessions, puts the agent into one real shop or clinic, where it either moves the number or it doesn't.

Detailed tools, platforms and program content will be shared soon.

## The Freshers track

The My AI Native Town Freshers track starts with how software is made: the software development life cycle (requirements, design, build and test, deploy and maintain). Then students learn how an AI agent is put together, and build agents again and again — each one small, each one working for a real business — until building one is routine. Freshers walk out with agents they built, running.

## The Experienced track

The My AI Native Town Experienced track is for people who already build software and want something harder than a tutorial, engineer or not. The homework evaluation shows whether an applicant fits this track. Each student owns a named layer of the real town agent — a tool, a data path or a guardrail — and ships it. It goes live with their name on the commit, or it does not go live. Experienced students walk out with a shipped layer in production.

## Eligibility and requirements

My AI Native Town is open to engineers and non-engineers: engineering students in their final or pre-final year, and graduates of any 3-year or 4-year degree. There is no age limit. Students need a laptop and an internet connection, with no minimum laptop specification, and must attend live sessions on alternate days, online or offline.

## Who should not join

My AI Native Town is not for someone who wants a certificate more than a working agent, cannot show up live every other day and log their own work on the days in between for 90 days, expects to be handed goals instead of chasing them, or needs recordings.

## Outcomes and target salary range

My AI Native Town students leave with an AI agent that runs for someone other than them, a public build log with their name on it, and a realistic shot at roles paying ₹3–6 LPA. The ₹3–6 LPA figure is the pay range of the roles the program trains for; it is not a guaranteed salary or a guaranteed job. Target job titles and placement partners have not been announced yet.
