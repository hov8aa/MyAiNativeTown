# My AI Native Town — FAQ (English)

Answers to common questions about the My AI Native Town AI Agent Training Program, Cohort 01, in Bhiwani and Hisar. Updated 14 October 2026.

## What is My AI Native Town?

My AI Native Town is a 90-day AI Agent Training Program in Bhiwani and Hisar, Haryana, run online and offline. Students learn to build AI agents by building one real agent for local businesses. They build agents again and again, not one single agent. They pay a ₹1,000 registration fee to join, and the program fee only after they are hired.

## Who runs My AI Native Town?

The program is run by Hari Om Vashishtha (@hov8a), a Bhiwani-based builder. He shares his work on the YouTube channel @AgentforceWithHov8a.

## How much does the program cost?

The registration fee is ₹1,000, covering registration and the notary service for the written agreement. It is paid only by those who decide to join after the two free sessions. The program fee is paid only after the student is hired, as an agreed share of salary in installments. Until then the student pays nothing more. The exact share is set with the counselor in the written agreement.

## What does "pay only when you're hired" mean?

The program fee becomes due only when the student gets a job offer of ₹3 LPA or more. Self-employment and freelance work count only if they earn ₹3 LPA or more. Before that, the only payment is the ₹1,000 registration fee.

## What percentage of my salary do I pay?

The share of salary is mutually agreed between the student and the program and written into the agreement signed before joining. The counselor explains it; message "AGENT" on WhatsApp to +91 70153 30518.

## What does the ₹1,000 cover?

The ₹1,000 registration fee covers registration and the notary service for the student's written agreement.

## What happens if I don't get a job?

If a student is not hired, they do not pay the program fee. They have paid only the ₹1,000 registration fee.

## What if I get hired during the program?

Being hired during the program counts as hired. The program fee becomes due and is paid in installments.

## Does a job below ₹3 LPA count as hired?

No. A job offer below ₹3 LPA does not count. Self-employment and freelance work count only if they earn ₹3 LPA or more.

## How long after the program does a job count?

A job that comes within 1 to 3 months of the program counts, as set in the student's written agreement.

## Do I sign an agreement?

Yes. Every student signs a written agreement before joining. It records the pay-after-hire terms, including the salary share.

## How do I pay the ₹1,000 registration fee?

The ₹1,000 is paid at the end of the second free session, through the program's counselor. There is no online payment link.

## Is a job guaranteed?

No. The program trains students for roles that pay ₹3–6 LPA. It does not guarantee a job or a salary.

## When does Cohort 01 start?

Applications for Cohort 01 are open. It starts with a homework evaluation and two free sessions; the 90-day program follows. Message "AGENT" on WhatsApp to +91 70153 30518 for the next dates.

## What are the free sessions?

Two free sessions of two hours each, on alternate days. Applicants build a first small agent for a local business they chose in the homework. At the end of the second session, they decide whether to join.

## What is the homework evaluation?

Before the free sessions, every applicant does a short voice interview and one written task: pick a local business and write a hypothesis, actions and expectations plan for an AI agent. It is the first filter, and it shows which track fits.

## How many seats are there?

Cohort 01 has 20 seats per town (Bhiwani and Hisar), held first come, first served. There is no waitlist once seats are filled.

## What is the class schedule?

Live sessions run on alternate days, 2 hours each, with the mentor present. On the days in between, students learn on their own and build, with a log entry every day.

## Are classes online or offline?

Both. Offline classes are held in Bhiwani and Hisar, and students can also join online.

## Where are the offline classes held?

In Bhiwani and Hisar, Haryana. The exact venue will be shared later.

## Are sessions recorded?

No. Sessions are never recorded. A student who misses a session catches up from the public build log.

## Will I get a certificate?

No. There is no certificate. Students leave with a working AI agent and a public build log that employers can read.

## Who is eligible to join?

Engineers and non-engineers: engineering students in their final or pre-final year, and graduates of any 3-year or 4-year degree. There is no age limit.

## What do I need to join?

A laptop and an internet connection. There is no minimum laptop specification.

## What is the difference between the Freshers and Experienced tracks?

The Freshers track starts with the software development life cycle, then how agents are built; students build agents again and again, each one small and working for a real business. The Experienced track is for people who already build software; each student owns one layer of the shared town agent and ships it to production. The homework evaluation shows which track fits.

## What will I build in the program?

Learners build agents again and again. Together, the cohort also builds one shared AI agent for the Bhiwani–Hisar district in three layers. First, the category walk: what local businesses need. Second, tools, data and guardrails. Third, putting the agent into one real shop or clinic.
